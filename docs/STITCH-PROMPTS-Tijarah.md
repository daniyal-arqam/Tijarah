# Tijarah — Google Stitch prompts (copy one at a time)

Stitch ko **poori PRD mat do**. Ek prompt = ek screen.  
Design: **premium, unique, attractive** — simple / boring / generic SaaS nahi.

**Look name:** Molten House — Riyadh private metal trading house. Dark foundry + desert gold + Arabic geometry. Luxury industrial magazine, not a startup dashboard.

---

## Stitch pe kaise chalana hai (sirf yeh)

1. Chrome mein kholo: **https://stitch.withgoogle.com** (Google account).
2. **New project** → type **Web** (desktop website, app nahi).
3. Jo bada text box hai, usme **PROMPT 1** poora copy-paste karo → Generate / Send.
4. Screen aa jaye to **usko select karke** agla prompt do (PROMPT 2). Naya project mat banao.
5. Koi cheez galat ho to **ek chhoti follow-up** do (neeche “Fix prompts”). 5 cheezein ek saath mat likho.
6. 8–10 screens ke baad **Export to Figma**.

Aaj minimum: Prompt 1 → 2 → 3 → 4 → 5 → 6.

## Brand (har pehle prompt ke ANDAR pehle se likha hai)

- Colors: charcoal `#0E1110`, desert ivory `#F3E6D0`, molten copper `#C45C26`, antique gold `#C9A227`, brushed steel `#8A9A96`
- Arabic geometry (mashrabiya) sirf dark panels pe, halka
- Badi bilingual typography: **Tijarah / تجارة**
- No purple, no Inter-only SaaS, no 6 rainbow KPI cards, no cute illustrations, no GitHub button

# PROMPT 1 — pehle yeh paste karo (Landing English)

```
Design a stunning desktop web landing page (1440px) for Tijarah (تجارة), a luxury B2B metal trading house in Saudi Arabia. Salesmen/middlemen get orders from companies for steel, tanks, pipes, CNC, and custom metal, then place jobs with factories.

VISUAL (must feel expensive and unique, NOT a simple SaaS template):
Dark cinematic foundry atmosphere. Hero is almost full-viewport. Background: close-up of brushed steel and molten copper sparks, overlaid with a faint Arabic mashrabiya geometric pattern. Left side huge type: “TIJARAH” in a sharp editorial serif or condensed display, and under it Arabic “تجارة” in a refined Naskh-like UI font, gold #C9A227. Headline: “Metal, without another site visit.” Subhead: “Your public trust profile. Private factory cost. Company only sees the offer.”
Two dramatic CTAs as copper metal plates, not rounded pills: “I sell metal” and “My company buys”. Top-right: EN | ع language toggle and Log in as ghost gold text.
Right of hero: a floating luxurious quote certificate (thick paper, gold corner ornaments, line items, total 18,000 SAR) with ONE row redacted by a copper wax-seal lock labeled “Factory cost — only you”.

Below the fold, do NOT use 3 equal feature cards. Use an asymmetric editorial layout: a wide cinematic photo of a Jubail industrial skyline at dusk, overlapping a dark panel with 3 numbered steps in gold (01 Profile  02 Quote  03 Deliver). Footer on charcoal with gold hairline, cities: Riyadh · Jeddah · Dammam.

Attractive, bold, Gulf luxury industrial. High contrast. No purple, no Inter dashboard, no illustrations of cartoon people, no generic gradient blobs.
```
